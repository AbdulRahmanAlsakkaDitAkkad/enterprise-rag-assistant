Enterprise RAG Assistant - Demo Report

Project: Smart Warehouse Analytics
Reporting period: Q2 2026

The warehouse processed 12500 orders during Q2 2026, representing a 25 percent increase compared with Q1.

Average order processing time decreased from 48 minutes to 36 minutes after introducing automated inventory tracking.

The order accuracy rate reached 98.5 percent, compared with 96.2 percent in the previous quarter.

The main operational challenge was a shortage of warehouse staff during peak demand periods.

The management team recommends expanding automated inventory tracking, improving staff scheduling, and introducing weekly performance reviews.

The target for Q3 2026 is to reduce average processing time to 30 minutes while maintaining order accuracy above 98 percent.
